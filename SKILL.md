# Taste

*Taste*: Old French *taster*, to touch, to try by hand. Judgment earned by handling the thing. This skill holds what that handling has settled, and nothing else.

## Work

1. **Look.** Render it and judge what a person sees, not the source.
2. **Cut.** Remove what has not earned its place before adding anything.
3. **Fix the largest fault first.** Hierarchy, then spacing, then motion, then polish.
4. **Judge.** A new look is scored with the rubric in standard.md, one line of evidence per score, before and after. A small fix is checked against interface.md's Bare rules and its signs; the rubric costs more than a one-row fix is worth.
5. **Report** both judgments side by side, what changed and why, and what you left alone.

## Hold to

- A good screen is left alone. Say so, and offer at most three refinements with exact values.
- A new look is drawn and shown to Carter before it is built. He is the final judge.
- An approved design is built as drawn, look and motion. It is not the old screen nudged toward it.
- Quote Carter exactly. A rule that came from him carries his words and the date.
- Never rename the product, redraw its marks, or invent features, claims, copy or data.
- The score measures the work. It does not design it.
