# Taste

*Skill*: Old Norse *skil*, discernment, the power to tell things apart. This skill is that and nothing else: telling what belongs from what does not.

## Work

1. **Look.** Render the thing and judge what people see, not the source.
2. **Judge.** For a new look, score it with the rubric in standard.md, one line of evidence per score. For a small fix, check it against the Bare rules and the signs in interface.md; the rubric costs more than a one-row fix is worth.
3. **Cut.** Remove what has not earned its place. Do this before adding anything.
4. **Fix the largest fault first.** Hierarchy, then spacing, then motion, then polish.
5. **Judge again.** Report both judgments side by side, and what you did not change.

## Hold to

- A good screen is left alone. Say so, and suggest at most three refinements with exact values.
- A new look is drawn and judged before it is built. Carter is the final judge.
- An approved concept is built as designed, look and motion. It is not the old screen nudged toward it.
- The score measures the work. It does not design it.
- Never rename the product, redraw its mark, or invent features, claims or data.
