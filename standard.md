# Standard

*Standard*: Old French *estandart*, the flag a company rallies to. This is the flag.

## What good is

- **Exactly what it needs to be and nothing more.** A screen, a page, a name, a function. Every part has earned its place.
- **Plain, self-explanatory, complete.** A foundation should work like Montessori material: you see it and know what to do.
- **Premium, reserved, human.** Every size, colour and word is a decision someone made. Work that could belong to any product is not finished.
- **Quiet frame, one rich moment.** The chrome stays still. The care goes where the person is looking: a figure set well, a chart drawn well, a painting, real content.
- **Near perfect, not perfect.** Precision in the craft, life in the content. Sterile everywhere reads as a template.
- **Human scale.** Sizes, density and targets follow the hand and the eye.

## The nucleus

*Nucleus*: Latin for kernel, from *nux*, nut. To reach it, trim until only the thing itself remains.

## Judging

The rubric is Carter's framework, Adjudicating Beauty. Beauty is a signal of fertility: a thing looks beautiful when it shows it is alive, cared for and able to make more good things. Work shows this in three ways, and each is a product of two halves, so a zero on either side is a zero.

| Pillar | Asks |
| --- | --- |
| **Craft** = medium × execution | Is it made of the right material, and made well? |
| **Design** = temperature × harmony | Is there life in it, and is that life ordered? |
| **Content** = resonance × value | Does it reach the person, and is it worth reaching them with? |

The roots say what each half measures.

| Half | Root | Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Latin *medium*, the middle: what stands between maker and person | **M1** quality: authentic, hard to fake. **M2** suitability: the right form for the job |
| Execution | Latin *exsequi*, to follow out to the end | **E1** mastery: precise, no stray values. **E2** integrity: holds under real conditions. **E3** endurance: holds in a year |
| Temperature | Latin *temperare*, to mix in due proportion | **T1** detail at each distance. **T2** meaningful distinctions per area. **T3** curvature against the grid. **T4** hue intensity, held to a moment. **T5** hue contrast that means something |
| Harmony | Greek *harmonia*, a joining | **H1** balance on every scale. **H2** repetition and rhythm. **H3** self-similarity across scales. **H4** edges that line up across the whole. **H5** one colour family |
| Resonance | Latin *resonare*, to sound back | **R1** legibility at a glance. **R2** salience: what matters most stands out first. **R3** depth: it rewards a second look |
| Value | Latin *valere*, to be strong, to be worth | **V1** warrant: every element earns its place. **V2** contribution: it moves the work forward. **V3** reach: one person, a team, or wider |

Temperature and harmony follow Nikos Salingaros. Temperature is scored for fit, not amount: a dense table runs cooler than a sign-in page.

### Scoring

Score each criterion 0 to 4, with one line of evidence. A score without evidence does not count.

| Score | Means |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Absent or wrong |
| 1 | Weak, good by accident |
| 2 | Adequate, what a careful template gives |
| 3 | Good, clearly decided |
| 4 | Exceptional, worth showing someone |

```
pillar = (mean of first half / 4) × (mean of second half / 4) × 100
```

Scores run low on purpose: all 2s give 25, all 3s give 56. Under 30 is a template, 30 to 50 careful, 50 to 70 designed, over 70 rare.

**Scale cap.** Count the levels of size the eye picks out at one distance, such as panel, row, label. Fewer than two reads as blank and more than five as noise. Outside two to five, cap T1, T2, H1 and H2 at 2.

**Template cap.** Swap the product's name for another's. If the work still fits, cap T4, T5, R3 and V3 at 2.

Score before and after a change. Never change good work to raise a number.
